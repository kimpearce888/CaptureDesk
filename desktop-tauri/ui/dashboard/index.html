About CaptureDesk

CaptureDesk Desktop — professional local screen recording, editing and export. 100% on-device: recordings never leave your machine. MIT License · CaptureDesk Project.

Welcome to CaptureDesk

  1. Pick a source. Fullscreen, a single window, or a precise region.
  2. Tune the capture. Microphone, system audio, camera bubble, cursor & click highlights.
  3. Polish locally. Trim, annotate and export from CaptureDesk Editor — nothing is uploaded, ever.

Tip: press Ctrl+Alt+R anywhere to start or stop recording.
